The Logic Behind Impact

Impact isn't simple addition—it follows a dynamic mathematical curve.

When probability is near 50%, an Impact of +5 makes a massive jump. But as you approach 99% or 1%, the curve flattens out, meaning you need overwhelmingly strong evidence to push the needle further. The glowing dot shows exactly where your current prediction sits!
