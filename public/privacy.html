Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

At Ultimate AI Predictor, your privacy is our priority. Because of the way our serverless architecture is designed, we are proud to operate a zero-retention platform.

Data Collection

We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personally identifiable information (PII) to use our core prediction engine.

Data Processing

When you input a "Target Event" and "Evidence", that text is temporarily sent to our secure backend to be processed by our AI models. Once the probability is calculated and returned to your screen, the data is immediately discarded. We do not store your prompts, predictions, or session history on our databases.

Cookies and Tracking

We may use basic, non-intrusive analytics to understand overall site traffic and ensure our servers are running smoothly. If we integrate third-party advertising (like Google AdSense), those vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.
