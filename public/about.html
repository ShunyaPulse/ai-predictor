The Mathematics of Prediction

Our engine does not use random guesswork. It is built upon the principles of Bayesian Inference. When you input a target event, the system initializes at a state of maximum entropy (50% probability).

As you provide new evidence, our AI neural pathways evaluate the contextual weight of that data. We calculate a precise "Impact Score" and map it onto a logistic S-Curve (a sigmoid function). This means moving a probability from 50% to 60% requires moderate evidence, but pushing a prediction from 95% to 99% requires absolute, undeniable proof. This perfectly mirrors how logical certainty operates in physics and the real world.
