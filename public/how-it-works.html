How It Works

The Ultimate AI Predictor is designed to be highly intuitive while running complex calculations behind the scenes. Here is how you can simulate future scenarios.

Step-by-Step Example

1. Define the Target Event: Start by asking a clear, measurable question. For example: "Will humanity establish a permanent base on Mars by 2035?"

2. Provide the Evidence: Input data points one by one or in a batch. For example, you might input: "SpaceX successfully tested the Starship booster catch mechanism today."

3. Let the Engine Calculate: The AI will analyze your evidence, assign it a logical weight (Impact), and shift the probability along the S-Curve. It will also provide a 1-2 sentence justification for why the math shifted.

4. Iterate: Add counter-evidence (e.g., "Funding for space exploration was cut by 20% this year") to see the probability correct itself downwards. The more data you feed it, the more accurate the prediction becomes!
